Add a rule

Just respell it. GAAP said as gap, EBITDA said as ee bitda — ordinary letters, no phonetic alphabet. Only tick the IPA box for a word no spelling reaches.

A word matches whole words only. A phrase matches anywhere, so use it for things with a full stop like vs. A regex gives you lookarounds — that is how the month rules fire on Jul 2 but not on Julian.
